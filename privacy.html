Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Linux-Ai Calendar ("the app") is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a hosted service and is not used by anyone else.

What Google data the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission, calendar.app.created. With it, the app can create one secondary calendar ("Linux-Ai Market Events") and add, change and delete events in that calendar only. It cannot see or change any other calendar, event, email, contact or file in the Google account.

How the data is used

The app writes market events (earnings dates, ex-dividend dates, option expiry dates and economic release dates) into its own calendar, and reads back only the events it created so it can update or remove them.

Storage and sharing

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner.
  • No Google user data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server or third party operated by the app.
  • Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the "Linux-Ai Market Events" calendar removes everything the app created.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
